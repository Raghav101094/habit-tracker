## 🚀 Getting Started

### 1. Access Your App

Open your browser and go to: **http://localhost:3000**

### 2. Create an Account

1. Click "Get Started" button

## 📱 Features Overview

### ✅ Daily Habit Tracking

**What you can do:**
- Add new habits with custom icons and colors
- Check off habits as you complete them each day
- See real-time streak updates
- View monthly completion percentages

**How to use:**
1. **Add a Habit:**
   - Click "+ Add Habit" button
   - Enter habit name (e.g., "Meditation", "Workout")
   - Choose an icon from the emoji picker
   - Select a color for visual identification
   - Click "Add Habit"

2. **Track Daily:**
   - See all your habits on the dashboard
   - Click the checkbox to mark as complete
   - Watch your streak grow! 🔥

3. **Delete a Habit:**
   - Click the trash icon on any habit card
   - Confirm deletion

### 📊 Statistics & Analytics

**View Your Progress:**
- Current streak for each habit
- Monthly completion percentage
- 3-month calendar heatmap
- Green squares = completed days
- Red squares = missed days

**How to access:**
1. Go to dashboard
2. Click the "Stats" tab
3. Scroll through each habit's calendar view

## 💡 Tips for Success

### Building Streaks
- ✅ Check habits off DAILY to maintain streaks
- 🔥 Streaks motivate you to stay consistent
- 📈 Watch your completion percentage improve

### Best Practices
1. **Start Small:** Add 2-3 habits first, master them, then add more
2. **Morning Routine:** Check your dashboard first thing in the morning
3. **Evening Review:** Mark completed habits before bed
4. **Weekly Review:** Check your stats every Sunday

### Understanding Metrics

**Current Streak:**
- Counts consecutive days you've completed the habit
- Resets to 0 if you miss a day
- Shows "X day streak" on each habit card

**Monthly Completion:**
- Percentage of days you completed the habit this month
- Calculated as: (completed days / total days) × 100
- Updates in real-time as you check habits

## 📅 Calendar Heatmap Guide

The calendar heatmap shows your last 3 months of activity:

- 🟩 **Green:** Completed that day
- 🟥 **Light Red:** Missed that day
- ⬜ **Gray:** Future date (not yet reached)

**Reading the Heatmap:**
- Look for long green streaks (consistency!)
- Identify patterns (do you miss weekends?)
- Use it to stay motivated

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

### Habit not checking off?
- Make sure you're online
- Refresh the page
- Check if you're still logged in

### Stats not updating?
- Switch to "Today" tab and back to "Stats"
- Refresh the page
- Stats update after you check/uncheck habits

### Can't see my habits?
- Make sure you're logged in
- Check the "Today" tab (not "Stats")
- Try refreshing the page
