# Date Navigation Feature - User Guide

## ✅ New Feature: Track Any Day!

You can now update your habits for **any date** - past, present, or future!

---

## 🎯 How to Use

### Navigate Between Days

You'll see a date selector at the top of the "Today" tab with three controls:

```
← | [📅 Monday, January 13, 2026] | → [Today]
```

**Three ways to change the date:**

1. **Previous Day Button (←):**
   - Click the left arrow to go back one day
   - Keep clicking to go further back in time

2. **Next Day Button (→):**
   - Click the right arrow to go forward one day
   - Navigate to future dates if needed

3. **Calendar Picker (📅):**
   - Click the date text in the middle
   - A calendar popup will appear
   - Click any date to jump directly to it

4. **Today Button:**
   - Shows only when you're NOT on today's date
   - Click to instantly return to today

---

## 💡 Common Use Cases

### Forgot to Track Yesterday?
1. Click the **← (left arrow)** once
2. You'll see yesterday's date
3. Check off the habits you completed
4. Click **"Today"** button to return

### Tracking Last Week's Activities
1. Click the **📅 calendar icon**
2. Select the date from last week
3. Mark your completed habits
4. Use **← →** arrows to navigate day by day
5. Click **"Today"** when done

### Planning Ahead
1. Click the **→ (right arrow)** to go to tomorrow
2. You can mark habits you plan to do
3. Note: You'll see a **"⚠️ Future date"** warning

## 🔥 How Streaks Work with Past Dates

### Updating Past Dates
- ✅ You can check off habits for any past date
- ✅ Streaks will recalculate based on ALL your data
- ✅ Your monthly % will update automatically

### Example:
**Scenario:** You have a 3-day streak but forgot to track 2 days ago

**Before updating:**
```
Day 1: ✅ Completed
Day 2: ❌ Missed (forgot to track)
Day 3: ✅ Completed
Streak: Broken (0 days)
```

**After going back and checking Day 2:**
```
Day 1: ✅ Completed
Day 2: ✅ Completed (just updated!)
Day 3: ✅ Completed
Streak: 3 days! 🔥
```

## ⚠️ Important Notes

### Future Dates
- You CAN mark habits for future dates (planning ahead)
- You'll see: **"⚠️ Future date"** indicator
- These don't count toward streaks until that date arrives
- Useful for planning your week!

### Data Persistence
- All changes save immediately to the database
- You can update the same day multiple times
- Changes sync across all views (Today tab & Stats tab)

### Honest Tracking
- Remember: The goal is honest tracking for self-improvement
- Don't retroactively mark habits you didn't do
- Use this feature to fix genuine oversights, not to fake progress

## 🐛 Troubleshooting

### Date selector not appearing?
- Make sure you're on the "Today" tab (not "Stats")
- Refresh the page if needed

### Changes not saving?
- Check your internet connection
- Verify you're logged in
- Refresh and try again

### Streaks not updating?
- Wait a moment (calculations take ~1 second)
- Switch to Stats tab and back
- Refresh the page

## 📊 Impact on Statistics

### Streak Calculation:
- Counts **consecutive completed days**
- Recalculates when you update past dates
- If you add a missing day in the middle, streak extends!

### Monthly Percentage:
- Total completed days ÷ days in month
- Updates when you change any date
- Shows in real-time on habit cards

### Calendar Heatmap:
- Green squares for all completed dates
- Red for incomplete dates
- Updates immediately in Stats tab
